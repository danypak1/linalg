What a question looks like

Which statement is exactly what v · w = 0 tells you about two vectors?

AThey have the same length, whatever their directions
BThey meet at a right angle, if neither is zero
CAt least one of them must be the zero vector
DThey are scalar multiples of one another
✗ Not quite — the answer is (B) What this mistake means: that is the instinct from ordinary numbers, where ab = 0 forces one factor to vanish. Vectors do not work that way: (1, 0) · (0, 1) = 0 with neither vector zero. A dot product of zero is a statement about the angle, and it costs one multiplication to test.
